What these numbers don't claim:

  • G1 (≥ 0.70 macro, ≥ +0.15 over the 0.4331 floor) is NOT MET. The best published arm is 0.5339 = +0.10080873944405344 over that floor, and its own harness marks the run VOID; the complete run G1 was judged on is V1-B at 0.4893 = +0.056262402214293905. Neither clears it.
  • The 0.70 target is gated, not promised
  • Training closed at v0. The Kaggle lane that produced V1-B is now measured closed for this account — five request variants all come back CPU (SPEC.md §9.53(vi)) — and the paid-GPU plan was reversed: v1 scaling is free-only, so nothing on this page will move without a new free accelerator.
  • No measurements on larger batches, longer sequences, or multilingual data

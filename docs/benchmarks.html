All three runs share one replicate set — same 1,176 rows, same --seed 20261031, first-replicate digest d45e5c018ee550da in all files — so the 0.0504 that separates the two competitors is arithmetic on paired points with no interval, and is not plotted. The v6 row is not a like-for-like arm: it was fine-tuned on decision-v2-pilot/train.jsonl, so every one of the 16 cells is inside its training distribution, while laya and vegaml are zero-shot on 14 of them. Its +0.1956 over laya is a trained-versus-untrained gap. It is also a different artifact from the two gates below it — G2's speed and G3's browser build are measured on the 14.45M-parameter v0 engine, which fails G1 and G5. The vegaml macro is forced-choice: 631 of its 1,440 outcomes (43.8%) carry its own decline flag and are scored anyway, because neither myna nor laya can decline; of the 809 it answers it is right 548 times (0.6774), of the 631 it declines 292 (0.4628). A second, independent flag — unbound, its calibration's distance-from-manifold test — fires on 261 of the 1,440 (18.1%), 178 of them also declined; recorded, not applied. Its views is pinned to 1. The cell where myna leads laya (imdb/positive, +0.3375, p=4.63e-07) is −0.0750, p=0.180 against the control: no competitor read gives this checkpoint a cell it wins with statistical support.

What these numbers don't claim:

  • G1 (0.70 accuracy) is met on v6 and on a borrowed trunk: 0.8620 macro against the 0.4331 floor, but 434,012,160 of its 446,631,182 params are a fine-tuned deberta-v3-large and all 16 cells are in-domain — the chart's Kaggle arm stays at 0.4893 [0.4647, 0.5139], +0.0563 [0.0192, 0.0676], not met
  • G4 and G6 are not dose questions: v6 has no state beyond a 512-token truncation for the needle ladder to probe, and bench/eval_synthetic.py cannot load a checkpoint that is not the SSM
  • Neither competitor comparison is a parity claim, and the 0.6160 control is a same-author design scored forced-choice, not a leaderboard entry
  • The 1.799 GB of v6 weights are not in git — they are the public Hub repo Aashish254/myna-v6 (12 files, verified against its JSON API on 2026-11-02), so every v6 command here needs that download first and a clean checkout re-runs none of them without it
  • No measurements on larger batches, longer sequences, or multilingual data
